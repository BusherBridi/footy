# Footy

An arcade, Rematch-style American football game made in Godot 4. Each player controls one athlete in online 5v5 tackle football.

Status: step 4 in progress. **Play vs bots** (menu, or `godot --path godot -- --match`) runs 5v5 plays against AI bots: snap (E), rush timer, line of scrimmage, tackles, turnovers and touchdowns. You are the QB on offense and the linebacker on defense. `--autoplay` lets the AI play your athlete too (watch mode). The old free-roam testing mode is **Host sandbox** (`--host`). Downs and scoring come next (4b).
